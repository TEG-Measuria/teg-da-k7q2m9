VisiOffice 3: running a measuring session

9 minutes · Systems

Purpose

After this lesson you can run a VisiOffice 3 measuring session in the right order and give the order everything it needs. Good measuring is what makes a personalised lens perform for the wearer.

Worked example

Mrs V, 57, solicitor, is having personalised varifocals. She has chosen her frame and you have adjusted it on her face.

  1. Create her file with name and surname.
  2. Clip the measuring clip on the frame, centred on the bridge, with the frame resting against the back of the clip. It stays on until all measurements are done.
  3. She stands behind the floor line and looks at herself in the mirror.
  4. Take the far vision measurements, front photo and three-quarter photo.
  5. Check the markers and the corneal reflections, and the boxing.
  6. Take the near vision measurements: dominant eye, reading distance and near vision behaviour.
  7. Review the results and submit the session, ready to import into the order.

The lesson

Rule. VisiOffice on every Essilor patient.

Steps (from the training video notes).

  1. New client: name and surname, then open the file.
  2. Tap Frame choice, or Measurements if the frame is already chosen. A laser line shows on the floor.
  3. The client stands behind the line and looks at themselves in the mirror. The camera follows their eye height only if they look in the mirror.
  4. Adjust the frame properly first. Fit the clip with its central notch on the bridge centre. The clip stays on, untouched, until all far and near measurements are done.
  5. Far vision: choose Measurements, then Far vision, then H3D if licensed. The client follows the on-screen head movements. The software picks the most natural posture.
  6. Take the front photo. Check the clip markers are detected and both eyes are open.
  7. Take the three-quarter view: the client turns the head about 20 degrees (15 to 25 accepted) to a target, then looks back at the mirror without moving the head.
  8. Check the corneal reflections: the cross sits on the reflection in both views. The flash button confirms it is the camera flash.
  9. Boxing: lines go on the inner edge of the front of the rim, ignoring the groove.
  10. Far vision results: per-eye PD, heights, ERC, total PD, wrap angle, head cape, pantoscopic tilt, DBL and frame sizes.
  11. Near vision: dominant eye, reading distance, and near vision behaviour on the app.
  12. A second pair is measured separately with Add an equipment.

Coaching. The patient's cooperation decides the quality. Explain each step, keep the client relaxed and watch for blinks and head movement.

Check before you finish. Both eyes open, markers found, reflections right, boxing correct. Retake rather than accept a doubtful photo.

Personalisation. Higher personalisation levels use more of these values, so wrong inputs waste the lens.

If you cannot run a session. Use the fallback in the eyecode lesson and note why.

These steps come from training-video notes. Check with your practice manager: the VisiOffice 3 screens on your device and how sessions are submitted.

Watch-outs

  • Removing or touching the clip before all measurements are done.
  • Accepting a photo where the client moved their head.
  • Placing boxing lines on the groove instead of the rim edge.
  • Skipping near vision for a lens that needs it.

Tips

  • Practise the instructions out loud before the client arrives.
  • Ask the client to look at the mirror, not the screen.
  • Retake early rather than fix later.

Current practice notes

  • VisiOffice on every Essilor patient (TEG rule).
  • Steps are summarised from training-video notes and are not yet checked on the live device.
  • Check with your practice manager: how each practice submits the session so it appears in the UMS list.

Drill

Put in order: front photo, three-quarter photo, boxing, near vision, fit the clip.

Show model answer

fit the clip; front photo; three-quarter photo; boxing; near vision.

Today on the floor

Run a VisiOffice session on a colleague (not a patient) with your registrant or coach watching. Say aloud each step as you do it: laser line, mirror, clip on the bridge, far vision, front photo, three-quarter view, boxing. Your coach checks the order and what you say to the colleague.

About 5 minutes, with a registrant supervising.

What your coach looks for

  • Adjusts the frame first and fits the clip centrally
  • Coaches the colleague to look in the mirror and sit naturally
  • Checks the photo for clip markers and open eyes
  • Leaves the clip on until all far and near measurements are done

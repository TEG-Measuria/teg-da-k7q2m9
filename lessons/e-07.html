Essilor ordering in Opsysweb: the order form

9 minutes · Systems

Screen pictures coming soon. These steps come from our written guides. If your Optix One (desktop) screen looks different, follow your manager and tap Report a wrong step.

Purpose

After this lesson you can find your way round the Essilor order form in Opsysweb and fill in a draft up to SAVE. You will know which parts are yours and which only a trained person does.

Worked example

Mr N, 55, joiner, has been dispensed an XR Design varifocal in Optix One (desktop) and measured in VisiOffice. Now the order must go to Essilor.

  1. Log in to Opsysweb with your practice's own login (never share or write it down in notes).
  2. Start a New order.
  3. Choose IMPORT MEASUREMENTS and pick Mr N's session from the list.
  4. Work down the sections: Shape, Prescription, Thickness & measurements, Personalisation, Lenses and Summary.
  5. Tick Eyecode in the patient section.
  6. Press CALCULATE, check the preview, then SAVE.
  7. Stop. A trained person checks and sends.

You prepared a saved draft. You did not send it.

The lesson

What Opsysweb is. Essilor's ordering website. Jobs are dispensed in Optix One (desktop) first; Essilor orders are then placed in Opsysweb. Essilor order numbers start with RX.

The order form. New order, then these sections in order:

  1. Shape: the frame shape and type.
  2. Prescription: sphere, cylinder, axis, add and prism for each eye. Check it twice against the dispense.
  3. Thickness & measurements: PDs, heights, and the thickness choices.
  4. Personalisation: the position-of-wear values (see the personalisation lessons).
  5. Lenses: the design, index, coatings, colour.
  6. Summary: the whole order on one screen.

Buttons. IMPORT MEASUREMENTS fills the form from a VisiOffice session. CALCULATE works out the lens thickness. PREVIEW shows the result. SAVE stores the draft. SEND places the order.

Who presses SEND. Only a trained person sends an order. Do not send as part of practice or training.

Reference. Put the tray number, last name, first initial and Optix ID in the reference box so the job can be traced.

Eyecode. Tick Eyecode in the patient section for every eligible lens. The next lesson explains which.

After the order. The confirmation gives an order number starting RX. Record it in Optix One (desktop) and move the order's status on.

These are screen steps from documents. Check with your practice manager: the current Opsysweb screens and the order status names.

Watch-outs

  • Sending an order when you are not trained to.
  • Sharing or writing down a login.
  • Skipping the Eyecode tick for an eligible lens.
  • Typing measurements when a VisiOffice session exists.
  • Forgetting to record the RX order number.

Tips

  • Check the prescription against the dispense twice before you save.
  • Use the reference format every time.
  • If a field looks odd, stop and ask.

Current practice notes

  • Order form sections: Shape, Prescription, Thickness & measurements, Personalisation, Lenses, Summary; buttons IMPORT MEASUREMENTS, CALCULATE, PREVIEW, SAVE and SEND.
  • SEND is done only by a trained person.
  • Essilor order numbers start RX; the reference is tray number, last name, first initial and Optix ID.
  • Check with your practice manager: Glass & Glass account access and the current screens.

Drill

Write the six sections of the order form in order, and name the button that is not yours to press.

Show model answer

Shape, Prescription, Thickness & measurements, Personalisation, Lenses, Summary. SEND is for a trained person only.

Today on the floor

With your manager watching, open Opsysweb on a draft or training order. Without pressing SEND, point to the six sections of the order form in order, then to IMPORT MEASUREMENTS, CALCULATE, PREVIEW and SAVE. Say which button only a trained person presses.

About 4 minutes, with your coach.

What your coach looks for

  • Names the six sections in order
  • Says only a trained person presses SEND, and does not press it
  • Points to the reference box and says what goes in it
  • Does not use a real patient's details
